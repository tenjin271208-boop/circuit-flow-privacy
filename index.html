Circuit Flow — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

In short

Circuit Flow does not collect, store or sell any personal information. Your progress is saved only on your own device. On Poki, ads and analytics are handled by Poki under its own privacy policy.

1. Who we are

Circuit Flow is a free browser game made by an independent developer, TenJin (“we”). This policy explains what happens to your data when you play it.

2. Data we collect

None. The game has no account, no login, no chat and no server of its own. We never ask for your name, email address, age, location or any other personal information, and we do not use cookies or tracking of our own.

3. Data stored on your device

To keep your progress, the game uses your browser's local storage (localStorage). It contains only game data:

  • your save (money, vehicles, worlds, trophies, collection…) and a backup copy;
  • your settings (language, sound, music, reduced motion).

This data never leaves your device and we cannot see it. You can delete it at any time by clearing the site data (cookies and site data) for the website where you play, in your browser settings.

The “Export save” button copies your save as a text code to your clipboard, and “Import save” reads a code you paste yourself; nothing is sent anywhere.

4. Leaderboard rivals

The rivals shown in the game's rankings are computer-controlled characters. They are not real players, and no information about you is shared with other players.

5. Playing on Poki

When you play Circuit Flow on Poki, the game uses the Poki SDK to show advertisements (including optional rewarded videos) and to report technical game events (for example, when gameplay starts or stops). Poki and its advertising partners may use cookies and process data as described in Poki's privacy policy, which also explains your choices and rights. We do not receive any personal data from Poki.

6. Other versions

Versions of the game hosted elsewhere (for example on itch.io) contain no ads and make no network requests of their own. The hosting website may apply its own privacy policy.

7. Children

Circuit Flow is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no personal information at all, we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 (or under the age required by local law).

8. Your rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. The game data on your device is fully under your control (see section 3). For data processed by Poki, please use the options described in Poki's privacy policy.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
